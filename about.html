About Me

Ryley McLean

I am Ryley McLean, a 17-year-old aviation photographer based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. My passion for aviation photography comes from capturing the aircraft, people, and moments that make aviation unique. Through my photography, I aim to showcase the beauty, power, and precision of aircraft from around the world.

Alongside photography, I am a proud father to my son Grayson. My journey in aviation photography continues to grow as I balance my passion for aviation with building a professional portfolio and connecting with the aviation community.

Photography Experience

My aviation photography has been published on JetPhotos , one of the world's largest aviation photography databases. I continue to expand my portfolio through photography at airports, airshows, and aviation events across Canada.

I have also had the opportunity to collaborate with aviation photographer @kaip1776 (Kai Park) on Instagram, working together to create and share aviation photography content.

Photography Equipment

I use professional Canon camera equipment and aviation-focused lenses to capture high-quality images suitable for online publication, portfolios, and prints.

Camera

Canon EOS 90D
A 32.5 megapixel APS-C DSLR camera featuring Canon's Dual Pixel CMOS autofocus system, 45-point all cross-type autofocus, 10 frames per second continuous shooting, and 4K video capability. The high-resolution sensor provides detailed images while the fast autofocus system helps capture aircraft during takeoff, landing, and high-speed maneuvers.

Lenses

Sigma 150-600mm f/5-6.3 DG OS HSM Contemporary
A long-range telephoto lens designed for wildlife and aviation photography. The 150-600mm focal range allows me to capture aircraft from significant distances, while optical stabilization helps produce sharp images when shooting handheld.

Canon EF 50mm f/1.8 STM
A compact prime lens with a wide f/1.8 aperture, providing excellent low-light performance and sharp images. This lens is useful for aircraft details, portraits, and creative aviation photography.

Canon EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS II
A versatile standard zoom lens offering a wide-angle to short telephoto range. This lens is useful for capturing aircraft on the ground, airport environments, and wider aviation scenes.

My Goal

My goal is to continue developing as an aviation photographer, create meaningful images, collaborate with others in the aviation community, and share the excitement of flight through photography.
